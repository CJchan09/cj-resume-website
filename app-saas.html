How we start

One problem. A clear scope.一个问题，清楚的范围。

  1. Explain the users, current workflow and the problem you want to solve.说明使用者、现有工作流程和想解决的问题。
  2. Agree the essential screens, data and first-version scope.确认必要页面、数据与第一版范围。
  3. Review a prototype, then proceed with development, checks and handover.先审阅原型，再进行开发、检查与交接。

Before we build

Questions to settle first.开发前先说清楚。

Can we start with a small prototype?可以先做小型原型吗？

Yes. A focused prototype can clarify the key workflow before the project expands.

可以。先用聚焦的原型确认主要流程，再决定是否扩展。

Are hosting and integrations included?托管与系统连接都包含吗？

They are defined in the agreed scope. Hosting, third-party services and ongoing maintenance are discussed separately.

按确认范围安排。托管、第三方服务与后续维护分别讨论。
