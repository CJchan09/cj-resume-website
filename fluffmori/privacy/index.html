Privacy Policy

Your quiet stays yours

Privacy notice · 29 September 2026

No account. No tracking.

Fluffmori does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The Android app has no internet permission, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Your selected pet and preferences are saved only on your device. These include sound, music, vibration, movement and cat voice settings, plus whether you have tried petting. Touch positions are used in memory to animate the fur and are not saved.

Sounds and permissions

All sound recordings and artwork are bundled in the app. The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location. Sound stops when the app leaves the foreground or loses audio focus.

Your choices

Change your preferences in Settings. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local settings. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

If you contact support by email, your email provider sends your message to CJ Chan. Your address and message are used only to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send sensitive information.

Fluffmori is intended for adults seeking a short, playful break.
